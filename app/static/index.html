BEHIND THE ROUTES

One problem. Two ways to solve it.

Each customer goes to van A or van B. Both vans can carry seven load units. The aim is to keep customers close together and balance the loads.

  1. Classical search checks all 64 assignments and finds the exact benchmark.
  2. QAOA uses a 12-qubit simulator and a classical optimizer to search a penalized assignment model.
  3. Exact routing finds the shortest stop order for each van after the assignment is chosen.

The map shows those real assignments. Moving vans illustrate each route; they do not simulate traffic or travel time. The optimizer chart shows real energy evaluations, not a live sequence of route discoveries.

New experiments are stored only for this app session. The original submission results stay unchanged. Sampler measurements are stochastic, so repeated runs can differ.
